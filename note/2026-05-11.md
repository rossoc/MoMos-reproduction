I am much more curious to investigate the phi function, because I think that it
has strong connections with how a Neural Network works and so with model
compression and XAI.

On the other hand, I think that the two approach (\phi and VQ) can be linked
somehow. For example, I conducted a PoC fitting weights to the function 
$A \cdot (\exp(B \cdot i^C) - 1)$ to see if pre-fixing motifs could prevent 
collapse.
Preliminary tests show standard MoMos are more resilient than 2D blocks, with 
accuracy holding around 52–53% for a block size of 2 and 100 epochs of training.
Namely, the experiment I did fixes (256 * 2)^2 Motifs.

I believe using invertible functions could further optimize this.
