Defining the motifs $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times s}$, such that $k$ is the number of motifs and $s$ is the block size;
and the mosaic $M \in [k]^{n/s}$ for $s \mid n$, we can simplify a neural network with parameters $W$ as:

$$
W \approx \phi_Z(Z[M])
$$

Where $Z[M]$ corresponds to the `torch` operation of assigning the motifs $z_i \in Z = (z_1, ..., z_k)$ at the index indicated by $M = (i_0, ..., i_{n/s})$.
And $\phi_Z$ is a reshaping function that reassemble the weights $Z[M]$ so that they have the same shape as $W$, namely, it depends by the motifs shape, and the output shape.

---

## Hierarchical Motifs

For $s' \mid (n / s)$, there exist $M' = (i_0 \oplus ... \oplus i_{s'}, ..., i_{n / s - s'} \oplus ... \oplus i_{n / s})$, such that: 

$$
W \approx \phi_Z(Z[M]) = \phi_{Z^{s'}}(Z^{s'}[M'])
$$

---

## Complexity

$$
C(W) \leq l(W) = n q
$$

$$
C(Z, M) \leq l(Z) + l(M) = ksq + n / s \lceil \log_2(k) \rceil
$$
$$
\geq ksq + \frac{n}{s s'} \lceil  \log_2(k^{s'}) \rceil = ksq + \frac{n}{s s'} \lceil s' \log_2(k) \rceil
$$
$$
= l(Z) + l(M') \geq C(Z, M')
$$

---

## Simplification of Hierarchical Motifs

Given $\pi: [k'] \rightarrow [k]^{s'}$ we can approximate 

$$
W \approx \phi_Z(Z[M]) = \phi_{Z^{s'}}(Z^{s'}[M']) \approx \phi_{Z^{s'}}(Z^{s'}\pi[M_2])
$$

For $M_2 \in [k']^{n / (s \cdot s')}$.

---

## Bounding&nbsp;$k'$

The following bounds stand for $k'$ such that $\phi_{Z^{s'}}(Z^{s'}[M']) = \phi_{Z^{s'}}(Z^{s'}\pi[M_2])$:

$$
k' \leq k^{s'}
$$
$$
k' \leq n / (s \cdot s')
$$

---

## $\pi$

Let's define $Z' \subseteq Z^{s'}$ such that $Z' = [Z_1, ..., Z_{s'}]$ for $Z_i \in Z$.
In this way $\pi$ is a function that maps indexes on the elements of $Z'$ to indexes of elements of $Z^{s'}$. 

In particular I define $Z_i = \{Z[\iota_{i_1}], ..., Z[\iota_{i_{k' / s'}}]\}$,
thus $|Z_i| = (k' / s')$.
In this way:

$$
\pi(a) := \bigoplus_{i = 1}^{s'} Z_i \left[ \left\lfloor \frac{a \mod (k' / s')^{s' - i}}{(k' / s')^{i - 1}} \right\rfloor \right]
$$

---

## Summary

Basically, in MoMos each entry of $M$ is given by $i \in [k]$;
in hierarchical MoMos each entry of $M$ is given by $\iota \in [k' / s']$.
With the bounds on $k'$:

$$
k' \leq k^{s'}
$$
$$
k' \leq n / (s \cdot s')
$$

because bigger values of $k'$ translates in higher complexity with equal description power. 

---

## Implementation

I am currently running experiments with the following baseline MoMos: motifs
shape `(1, 2)` and capacity $0.001$, yielding about 500 motifs, achieving a
baseline accuracy of $53.7\%$.

At half training I instantiate a hierarchical motif, where I set the number of
motifs in each bucket $k' / s'$ as:

$$
k' / s' = \max(1, \min(n / (s \cdot s') \cdot capacity, k^{s'}))
$$
