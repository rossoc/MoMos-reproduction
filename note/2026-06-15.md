## Final Chapter

Several limitations qualify the results above and mark directions for future
work.

\paragraph{The projection is a heuristic with no convergence guarantee.}
Both training loops alternate gradient steps with a greedy nearest-motif
projection (\ref{alg:momos-training}, \ref{alg:vfold-training}). We give no
argument that this alternation converges, nor that the projection is optimal in
any sense; moreover, the motif dictionary is re-sampled randomly at each epoch,
so the projection target itself shifts between epochs.

\paragraph{The fold-construction heuristics are unablated.}
The capacity rule $k' = \lru{k \cdot c'}$, the frequency-weighted
sampling of motifs, and the choice to always include the zero motif in every
fold are design decisions adopted without ablation. Their sensitivity, and
whether simpler or alternative rules would perform comparably, is unknown.

\paragraph{The MoMos-2D null result.}
As reported above, MoMos 2D did not improve compression efficiency over the 1D
variant; performance clustered according to the block size $s$, suggesting that
exploiting spatial layout offers no benefit beyond what the raw
Kolmogorov-complexity bound already captures.
